What to expect

Guided from day one

Every ceremony is written from scratch and tailored to you.

I work with countryside barns, private homes, outdoor spaces and both licensed and non-licensed locations.

The process is guided from first conversation to the ceremony itself, with room for reflection and edits.

The style can be relaxed, traditional or contemporary, but the focus is always on creating something genuine and personal.

You do not need every answer on day one. I will guide the process, keep things organised and support the planning.
